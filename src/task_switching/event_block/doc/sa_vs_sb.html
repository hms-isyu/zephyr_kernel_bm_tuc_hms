Compiler
arm-none-eabi-gcc 14.2.1, -Og, -mcpu=cortex-m33 -mthumb -mabi=aapcs

CONFIG_SCHED_SIMPLE=y, CONFIG_WAITQ_SIMPLE=y, CONFIG_SMP=n, CONFIG_TIMESLICING=n, CONFIG_EVENTS=y. The queue here is the event’s wait queue; CONFIG_SCHED_MULTIQ replaces only the run queue.
Extraction
objdump -d --no-show-raw-insn of build_block/zephyr/zephyr.elf. Every row checked against it; every step a legal CFG successor.
Path
Derived, not recorded. Branch outcomes come from an explicit decision table (gen/blk_cfg.py), and the PendSV switch into L_Task_SA(7) is declared there. A legal path is not proof of the executed path.

SIM_Z_SWAP_PROLOGUE 11; wait_tail_no_wait 9; SIM_WAIT_PROLOGUE 95, of which 46 (add_to_waitq_locked 21, unready_thread 25) are stubbed in sim_sched.c and unmeasured.
