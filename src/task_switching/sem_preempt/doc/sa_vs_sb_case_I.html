Run-queue asymmetry
Wherez_priq_simple_add() (kernel/include/priority_q.h), inlined into ready_thread() under z_ready_thread(), called by the waiter arm of z_impl_k_sem_give() in both scenarios, before reschedule(). The loop walks the run queue until it finds a thread of lower priority than the one inserted.
S_AH_Task_SA(2) outranks the running L_Task_SA(7) at the head: one compare, insert at the head. 0 scan passes.
S_BL_Task_SB(10) ranks below the running H_Task_SB(8): the walk passes it and inserts before idle. 1 scan pass of 17 instructions (shaded rows).
ConsequenceThe stretch before reschedule() is not the same in the two windows. S_B carries 17 more instructions there, which no simulated tail removes, so S_A - (S_B - T_give) comes out short by the cost of that scan pass. The design cannot avoid it under CONFIG_SCHED_SIMPLE: the woken thread in S_A outranks the giver and goes to the head; the woken thread in S_B ranks below the giver and passes it.

Extraction
arm-none-eabi-objdump -d --no-show-raw-insn of the linked zephyr.elf. A walker follows fall-through, b, bl, bx lr and pop {.., pc} itself; the text of every row is read back from the listing.
Alignment
Caller rows and the rows from STOP on are paired role by role; the rows between are paired by a sequence diff over (function, offset).
Path
From the binary: instructions, addresses, branch targets, call and return order. Assumed: the outcome of each conditional branch, from the state stated above: wait queue holds the woken waiter only; its timeout is not linked and not being announced; it is not queued and not active elsewhere; run queue contents as in the table; _current is runnable and preemptible; key 0, thread context; S_A switches at the msr BASEPRI in z_swap_irqlock() and H_Task_SA resumes on the following isb. Not measured: nothing on this page was recorded on hardware.
Cost
Instruction counts only. Arm publishes no per-instruction cycle table for Armv8-M, so cost has to be measured differentially on the target.
